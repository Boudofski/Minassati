# Minassati Image Prompts

CSS-first illustrations only. Do NOT take images from Tawjeeh.ma, 9rayti.com, or any institution.
Do NOT use school logos or recognizable building photos. Use icons, gradients, and SVG illustrations.
Use these prompts only for generating original AI illustrations when needed.

## 1. Hero — Student Choosing Path

Flat illustration, Moroccan student silhouette at a crossroads with multiple directional signs (ENSA, Université, BTS, Abroad), warm green and red color palette matching Moroccan flag, compass motif, clean minimalist style, no face detail, RTL composition.

## 2. School Comparison Dashboard

Abstract dashboard card layout, three school-type cards side by side in Arabic RTL layout, emerald and slate colors, comparison checkmarks, no real logos or institution names, geometric flat style.

## 3. Study Abroad Planning Board

Top-down flat illustration of a planning board: world map outline in background, document stack (passport shape, transcript sheet, CV page), language book icon, calendar. Flat vector style, warm earth tones, no real country flags or embassy logos.

## 4. Scholarship Document Checklist

Organized set of flat document icons: passport outline, transcript lines, CV layout, envelope for motivation letter, arranged in a vertical checklist. Emerald checkmarks, clean white background, flat icon system style.

## 5. Moroccan Guidance Visual

Abstract aerial-view illustration of a Moroccan city scene: generic building outlines, generic school pin markers, student silhouettes walking paths. Soft warm colors, no identifiable landmarks, no logos, no specific architecture.

## 6. Article Cover System (CSS-only)

Implementable in Tailwind — no external image needed:
- Background: `bg-[linear-gradient(135deg,#b91c1c,#0f7a3b)]`
- Category badge: white pill top-right
- Title: white, right-aligned, font-black, RTL
- Bottom strip: white/10 opacity with reading time

## 7. After-Bac Path Navigator

Six path tiles arranged in a 2×3 grid: الجامعة, المدارس العليا, التكوين المهني, الخارج, المهارات الرقمية, إعادة التوجيه. Each tile has a small icon, emerald/slate color, subtle border. Student compass icon at top-center. Flat minimal vector style.
